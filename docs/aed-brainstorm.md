# Aed, the dragon sorcerer: a new opponent (brainstorm, 10 Nov)

The user's idea, 10 Nov: a new opponent called Aed, a dragon sorcerer, his ability drawn from the
sorcerer class in D&D 5e. Nothing here is built yet; the user chooses.

## What a 5e sorcerer has to draw on

- **Font of Magic / sorcery points:** a pool of points the sorcerer spends to bend spells.
- **Metamagic:** the sorcerer's signature. Ways to twist a spell, each costing points: *Quickened*
  (cast twice in a turn), *Twinned* (a spell hits a second target), *Heightened* (the target has
  disadvantage on its save), *Empowered* (reroll the damage dice), *Careful*, *Subtle*, *Distant*,
  *Extended*, *Seeking* (reroll a missed attack).
- **Draconic bloodline:** *Draconic Resilience* (tough scaly skin, extra hit points), *Elemental
  Affinity* (his dragon's element, fire for a red dragon, adds to his spells), *Dragon Wings* (he
  flies), *Draconic Presence* (an aura of awe or fear).
- Spells: *Chromatic Orb*, *Fireball*, *Chaos Bolt*.

## Ideas for his ability

1. **Metamagic, with sorcery points (recommended).** Aed has a pool of sorcery points for the fight
   (say 4), shown as glowing gems on his nameplate in place of the usual marks. He spends them on
   metamagic, and his brain (the solver, like the Yawning Portal's) picks when, so he plays like a
   clever caster:
   - *Heightened* (3 points): your next throw is made at disadvantage, each die rolled twice and the
     worse kept (the lost rolls rise as ghost dice, as with Double Bubble). The mirror of Stason's
     Vexing Rapier.
   - *Quickened* (2 points): he throws again without keeping a die, once.
   - *Empowered* (1 point): he rerolls one of his own loose dice he dislikes.
   - *Twinned* (2 points): a die he keeps is copied onto one of his loose dice (a kept 6 makes another 6).
   Each spend is its own moment, with a banner in his dragon's colours. It is the most sorcerer-like
   idea and like nothing else in the game: one opponent with a small menu of tricks rather than one move.
2. **Heightened Spell alone.** Once or twice a fight: your next throw at disadvantage. The simplest, and
   the clearest on screen, but only one corner of the class.
3. **Dragon's breath (Elemental Affinity).** Once a fight, after your throw, he breathes fire across the
   felt: your loose dice that landed 5 or 6 are scorched to 1s (or: your highest loose die melts to a 1).
   Very visual, but close to Vaelin's Inkfire Brand (which rerolls your 6s).
4. **Draconic Presence.** An aura of dread: while he's on his last heart, you must keep at least two dice
   from every throw (like the Squall). A phase like Jaguar's.
5. **Chromatic Orb.** Each round he picks an element and the round has its effect: fire (your 6s roll
   again), cold (keep two a throw), lightning (one loose die rerolled after you keep), acid (your
   finished hand -2), poison (your 1s stay 1s, no rerolls). A dial of his own, like the sea die.
6. **Draconic Resilience, alongside any of these.** His scales: he starts with one more heart, or the
   first heart he'd lose each fight stays (his own Shield).
