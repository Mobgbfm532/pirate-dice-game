# Water of the Ebon Shield: a use of its own (brainstorm, 10 Nov)

The user's question, 10 Nov: Jorvash's Water of the Ebon Shield does what the Lady's Favour does (one
extra heart next fight), so it needs a use of its own. Nothing here is built yet; the user chooses.

## Where it sits

Jorvash is a spot aboard the Salty Swallow, so the Water is bought between the ship's fights and works in
the next one: against Rokr, the Hangman or Jaguar. Those are the fights with the sea die, Starry Form,
Penitent Stare and the Ace, and Jaguar's three phases. His other drink, Notaro Red, already adds to your
score (+2 on your first hand that counts). So the Water is best as a *defence*: the shield in its name,
against something the ship does to you.

## Ideas

1. **Black water: the sea can't move your dice (recommended).** For the next fight the sea leaves your
   dice alone: no list to port or starboard on your throws, and a rogue wave never takes one of yours.
   The opponent still feels it all. It is the only thing in the game that answers the sea, it belongs
   aboard, and it is useless ashore, which is right for a drink only sold at sea. (Squall, Fog and
   Becalmed could count too, or not: the user's call. Counting all of them makes it strong on the
   Hangman's leg, where four faces of six are weather.)
2. **The ward: the opponent's next ability is turned aside.** The first time the opponent's ability would
   work on you next fight, it doesn't: like a Clear-Eye that works by itself. Simple and clearly a
   shield, but very close to the Clear-Eye Thonic.
3. **Ebon skin: the first heart you lose next fight to a lost round stays.** Like the Shield keepsake,
   once. Easy to understand, but it copies the Shield.
4. **The dark mirror: your first dust next fight costs them a heart too.** You still lose yours. A
   shield that bites back; it turns the worst hand into a trade.
5. **Last stand: on your last heart, your hands score 2 more.** Next fight only. A shield for when
   things are going badly; it never helps a fight that is already going well.
6. **The black cap: their first hand over 20 scores 20.** It blunts the opponent's best hand, once.
   Against the ship's opponents (who play their best) it bites often.

## Questions for the user
- Which idea, or a mix? (Recommended: 1, black water.)
- If 1: does it guard against only the list and the rogue wave, or the Squall, Fog and Becalmed too?
- Does the Water keep its name and painting? (The painting is a dark flask with a shield on it: it suits
  any of these.)
