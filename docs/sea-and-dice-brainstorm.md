# The sea die's needle, and the other opponents' dice (brainstorm, 13 Nov)

The user asked two questions on 13 Nov: what sound the needle of the sea die's dial could have, and
what the dice of every other opponent could look like. Both were built the same day from the user's own
clips and paintings (below). Also the same day, every
roll of the sea die got its wave: a held sea too, port breaking to the left and starboard to the right
(**Built**, 13 Nov).

## The needle's sound

**Built (13 Nov), with the user's own two clips:** a tick (`DialNeedle.mp3`) each time the needle sweeps past
a sector line and a clunk (`DialClunk.mp3`) as it first reaches its face. The ideas as they were:

The needle hunts while the die tumbles (about 1.1 s), then swings onto its face, overshoots twice and
settles (`dial-needle` in styles.css, 2.1 s in all).

1. **A brass ratchet (recommended).** Small dry ticks as it sweeps past each sector, faster as it
   swings and slower as it settles, then one solid brass *clunk* as it stops on the face. It reads as
   "measuring" and doesn't fight the wave's swell. It can be synthesized now (like the dice and the
   bell) and timed to the needle's own keyframes, so no clip is needed; a clip of the user's would
   replace it.
2. **A ship's engine-order telegraph.** Its handle's double ring-ring as the needle lands. Very
   nautical, but loud: it would sit on top of the swell, the foghorn and the news.
3. **A compass in oil.** A soft liquid whirr while it hunts and a small glassy *ting* as it settles.
   Quiet and pretty, but it may vanish under the wave on a phone speaker.
4. **A creak of the hull.** A wooden groan following the needle's swing, as if the dial were reading
   the ship's timbers. Moody, but it overlaps the swell's own creak.

## The dice of the other opponents

**Built (13 Nov), with the user's own ten sheets** (their own designs, not these ideas). The ideas as they were:

So far only the Ghoul and Gourd throw their own dice (Frak, Danhausen, the Duke, Martha; McGrath
throws yours). Every set keeps the house rule: **a red 1 and a green 6** (a 1 that blends in gets a
ring). Each is painted with the prompt in `docs/art-direction.md` ("An opponent's dice") and brought in
with the `dice-import` agent.

### The Yawning Portal
- **Sylvester Stonefist:** rough grey granite, chipped at the corners, the pips punched in like knuckle
  dents, dark iron in them.
- **Vaelin Inkhand:** ivory stained with ink, black ink bleeding along the edges, the pips pools of
  glowing violet ink (his Inkfire).
- **Stason Jatham:** polished duelling steel with a fine engraved filigree like a rapier's guard, the
  pips gold inlay.
- **The Ridgeler:** hammered temple bronze with a small holy sun stamped on each face, the pips pale
  gold light (Divine Smite).
- **Brodor:** goblin-made and proud of it: gaudy gold like a champion's belt, riveted, a little
  lopsided, the pips studs; a pair of tiny sunglasses engraved on the 6 (his drop).

### The Salty Swallow
- **Klarg:** carved from a big yellowed tooth or claw, crude knife-scratched pips rubbed with dirt.
- **Rokr:** pale driftwood with the pips as tiny silver stars joined by faint constellation lines
  (his hidden throws keep the night-sky dice).
- **Áéd:** faceted amethyst like a dragon's scale, violet with gold pips, a faint inner glow.
- **The Hangman:** black gallows wood, the pips bone white like bullet holes, a hairline crack across
  one face.
- **Jaguar Cantona:** amber with a jaguar's dark rosettes through it, the pips pearl; a captain's gold
  rim on the edges.

Painting one set a place at a time keeps the look even. Which come first is the user's call.
